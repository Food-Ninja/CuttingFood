Here's a grouping of the actions based on their parameterization in the context of cutting fruits and vegetables:

**Group 1: General Cutting**

* **Representative:**  `cut` 
* **Members:**  `slice`, `chop`, `divide`

These actions represent the most basic form of cutting, without specifying a particular shape or size.

**Group 2:  Precise Cuts**

* **Representative:** `dice`
* **Members:** `cube`, `julienne`, `mince`, `sliver`

These actions involve creating small, uniform pieces of a specific shape.

**Group 3: Halving/Quartering**

* **Representative:** `halve`
* **Members:** `quarter`

These actions involve dividing the item into equal halves or quarters.

**Group 4:  Peeling/Removing**

* **Representative:** `pare`

This action specifically refers to removing the outer layer (skin) of a fruit or vegetable.

**Excluded Actions:**

* **saw:**  While technically a cutting action, sawing is too forceful and imprecise for delicate fruits and vegetables.
* **carve:** Carving implies shaping or decorating, which is beyond the scope of basic food preparation.
* **snip:** Snipping is usually associated with cutting small, thin items like herbs, not larger fruits and vegetables.
